- 1st Keynote:
	Keynote speakers Marta Teperek (Center for Open Science, NL) & Dani Metilli (Ontology Engineer) gave a presentation about "Culture Change Across the Research Ecosystem"
	With reference to (this)[https://www.cos.io/blog/strategy-for-culture-change] blog post by Brian Nosek they pointed out the necessity to make infrastructure
	- required
	- rewarding
	- normative
	- easy
	- possible
	to make FAIR happen. 
	(They find the NFDI, especially the Section (Meta)data, Terminologies, Provenance amazing!)
	Especially addressing the reward systems by nudging towards FAIR principles proved to work well in the Netherlands. Small amounts eg. for implementing FAIR principles into the HR departments of institutions can make a huge difference. Missing evidence for the effectiveness of open science policies (such as DMPs) is still an issue, though. Clearer definitions of data reuse and stronger efforts to improve data interoperability across the whole research ecosystem are required.
