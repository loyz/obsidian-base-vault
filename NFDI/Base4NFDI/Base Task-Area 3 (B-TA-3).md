#B-TA-3
[Anmelden | NFDI4 OpenProject](https://openproject.nfdi.de/projects/ta3-service-coherence-processes-and-monitoring/)

## Main Goal: 
- set up coherent process
- monitor progress of individual services
- monitor appropriateness of selection process
- establish a framework that:
	- supports TA1 and TA2 in gathering consortia requirements for basic services
	- grants seamless operation of decision making process
	- compiles all information:
		- prescreen and prepare evaluation reports
		- ensure quality assurance of applications
	- support TEC and consortia assembly to carry out decisions
- SLOs directly support work of Sections in preparing:
	- development strategies
	- decisions
	- monitoring

## Measures:
3.1 Defining decision making process
3.2 Monitoring overall basic service progress
3.3 Preparing decision dossiers, decision-making templates for the 3 phases
