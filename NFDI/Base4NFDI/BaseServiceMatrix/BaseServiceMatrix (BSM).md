
It is a self-assessment tool for RDM services committed to the FAIR principles. It encourages deep integration into the NFDI and EOSC landscape. It is open to all non-commercial scientific services worldwide. 
The matrix represents the key achievements of the Base4NFDI framework for developing of basic services and makes them available to all participating RDM services. 
At the entrance level the (RDM-)BSM starts as a self-assessment tool, guiding to improve and assure service quality. 
To reach the highest level (Tier 3) the self-assessment has to be checked and approved by an external body (TEC). 
All services (unless they opt out of this feature) that register for the BSM will be listed in the BSM catalogue, which supports refined search options based on the information provided.

The BSM is a 3x3 Assessment Matrix that reflects a 3-stage progression in 3 Key Areas:
1) User and Community engagement/needs
2) Technical
3) Operations, Governance, Institutions

The 3 stages reflect the original phases of Base services, now mapped to the SRL:
1) TRL 3-5 (Initialisation)
2) TRL 6-7 (Integration)
3) TRL >7 (Ramp-Up)
