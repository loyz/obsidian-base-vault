A lot of communication about science is nowadays going on on commercial social platforms like LinkedIn. 
For science it would be much better, to have the scientists build their own social platform for scientific networking. 
It could be tailored for the needs of scientific research and the metadata from the communications could serve as a great monitoring tool to gain insights into the actual hot topics, networks, developments, needs and many other things that can be evaluated for better management.
For example it would allow to run opion polls with very accurate target audiences (e.g. scientists of a specific career stage and a specific specialisation).
The platform would follow the FAIR principles and for this reason it could also be employed to measure efficiency in science.
It would be open to public, while distinguishing between trained scientists and others.
It would serve very well to reflect the concept of scientific truth back into society.
