**Aims of the Base4NFDI Project**

The Base4NFDI project, supported by all 26 consortia of the Nationale Forschungsdateninfrastruktur (NFDI) and aligned with the goals outlined in the NFDI4Earth publication (Zenodo: 16736119), aims to deploy fundamental services in research data management and establish the backbone of a unified, federated national research data infrastructure. Our primary objectives are:

1. **Promote Open Science and Transparency**:
   - Embody the principles of open ideas and transparent processes (as per Zenodo: 13961033) to drive powerful scientific research.
   - Enhance accessibility to data and knowledge, fostering collaboration and accelerating innovation.
   - Create new economic opportunities by enabling industries to develop data-driven products and services (Zenodo: 16736119).

2. **Foster Interoperability and Cross-disciplinary Collaboration**:
   - Break down silos between disciplines and make data interoperable to facilitate the cross-pollination of ideas.
   - Spark new discoveries and enable more effective addressing of complex societal challenges.

3. **Respect Scientific Autonomy**:
   - Uphold the integrity of scientific findings by respecting scientific autonomy.
   - Work closely with researchers to understand their needs and support them in driving progress according to their own scientific standards.

4. **Advance Scientific Knowledge and Societal Well-being**:
   - Contribute directly to the well-being, prosperity, and competitiveness of society through advanced scientific knowledge.
   - Promote transparency in scientific processes and governance, building trust with citizens and ensuring the responsible use of public funds.

5. **Align with Policymaker Priorities**:
   - Actively communicate our progress and engage with policymakers to ensure our work aligns with their priorities.

We invite you to join us in this endeavor to shape a future where science thrives and benefits all. Together, we can advance research data management, foster innovation, and drive societal progress.
